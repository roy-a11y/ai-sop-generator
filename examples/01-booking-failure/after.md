# SOP: Handling a Failed Booking
**Version:** 1.0 (draft) | **Owner:** Not specified (see Open Questions) | **Last updated:** 25 Nov 2026

## 1. Purpose
Make sure that when a booking fails, the agent's money is either matched to a confirmed booking or refunded, and the agent is kept informed.

## 2. Scope
Used by the support team when a travel agent reports a failed booking by call or WhatsApp. Cases where a PNR is generated but the ticket is not issued follow a separate path (see Section 5).

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Support executive | Receives the agent's report, checks payment status, checks supplier status, informs the agent, logs the case |
| Finance team | Is told when money was deducted from the agent wallet |
| Supplier | Confirms or fails the booking, sometimes late |

## 4. Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Receive the report.** The support executive takes the failed-booking report from the agent by call or WhatsApp.
2. **Check payment.** The support executive checks whether money was deducted from the agent's wallet. (System or screen: Not specified.)
3. **Notify Finance.** If money was deducted, tell the Finance team.
4. **Wait for supplier confirmation.** The supplier may confirm the booking late, so wait before cancelling.
5. **Decide.** If the supplier confirms, the booking stands. If there is still no confirmation after the waiting period (about 1-2 hours, exact limit Not specified), cancel the booking and refund the agent.
6. **Inform the agent.** Tell the agent the outcome (confirmed or cancelled and refunded).
7. **Log the case.** Record the failure in the failures sheet.

## 5. Exceptions and Escalations
- **PNR generated but ticket not issued:** this is a different case from a failed booking. Contact the airline desk. (Contact and owner: Not specified.)
- **Money not deducted:** next action Not specified.
- **Escalation contact if Finance or the supplier does not respond:** Not specified.

## 6. Checklist
- [ ] Agent's report received and noted
- [ ] Payment deduction checked
- [ ] Finance informed (if money was deducted)
- [ ] Supplier status checked
- [ ] Booking confirmed, or cancelled and refunded
- [ ] Agent informed of the outcome
- [ ] Case logged in the failures sheet

## 7. Metrics
- Time from the agent's report to final outcome (confirmed or refunded)
- Share of failed bookings that are logged in the failures sheet
- Number of failures that are confirmed late by the supplier
