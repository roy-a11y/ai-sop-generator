# SOP: Onboarding a New Travel Agent
**Version:** 1.0 (draft) | **Owner:** Not specified (see Open Questions) | **Last updated:** 25 Nov 2026

## 1. Purpose
Get a new travel agent verified, activated and making a first booking quickly, ideally within the first week.

## 2. Scope
Covers sign-up through first-booking follow-up for a newly registered travel agent. Wallet top-ups are the agent's own responsibility and are out of scope.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Travel agent | Fills the sign-up form in the app, provides documents, adds money to their own wallet |
| Sales team | Calls the agent to verify business details |
| KYC team | Reviews documents and approves the account |
| Demo owner | Runs a demo call for the agent (owner Not specified) |

## 4. Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Agent signs up.** The agent fills the sign-up form in the app.
2. **Verify business details.** The sales team calls the agent to verify business details.
3. **Collect documents.** Collect the GST number and PAN. A shop photo may also be needed (to be confirmed).
4. **Approve KYC.** The KYC team reviews the details and approves the account.
5. **Activate the account.** Activate the account and send login details to the agent on WhatsApp and by email.
6. **Run a demo call.** Walk the agent through the platform. (Owner and timing: Not specified.)
7. **Follow up.** If the agent has made no booking 3 days after onboarding, follow up with them.

## 5. Exceptions and Escalations
- **Agent has not booked after 3 days:** follow up (Step 7). Further follow-ups: Not specified.
- **Verification or KYC fails:** next action Not specified.
- **Wallet is empty:** the agent adds money themselves. The company does not.

## 6. Checklist
- [ ] Sign-up form completed in the app
- [ ] Business details verified by a call
- [ ] GST number and PAN received (shop photo if required)
- [ ] KYC approved
- [ ] Account activated and login sent on WhatsApp and email
- [ ] Demo call completed
- [ ] Follow-up done if no booking by day 3

## 7. Metrics
- Share of new agents who make their first booking within 7 days
- Time from sign-up to account activation
